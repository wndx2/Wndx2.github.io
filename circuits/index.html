How it works

Building

Parts
Click one in the panel and click the page to put it down, or drag it straight on. Drag a part to move it; its wires follow.
Wires
Drag from the end of a part, the end of a wire, or anywhere along a wire. Wires join where their ends meet, and a junction gets a dot. Wires that only cross don't join, as on paper.
Turning and deleting
Select a part and press R to turn it, or Delete to remove it. Turning a cell or diode twice reverses it. R Delete ⌘Z
Moving around
Drag the empty page to pan. Pinch, or scroll with Ctrl held, to zoom.

Running

Readings
Meters show their reading beside them. Hover over any part to see the voltage across it, the current through it, its power and resistance; select it for a graph of voltage and current against time.
AC
With an AC supply in the circuit, meters read rms values, worked out over each cycle. The graph shows the instantaneous values, so the phase between voltage and current can be seen.
Speed
Real time suits most DC circuits. Slow it down to watch a 50 Hz supply cycle, or a fast capacitor charge; speed it up for slow ones. Reset empties the capacitors and mends blown fuses.

Parts

Cells and batteries
The long plate is positive. Give one an internal resistance to see the terminal voltage drop under load.
Meters
Ideal: an ammeter has no resistance and a voltmeter takes no current.
Lamps
Ohmic, with a rated power: they shine at full brightness when they take it, and blow if run at well over it. Switch on Blown in a lamp's panel to break it by hand.
LDR and thermistor
The LDR runs from 1 MΩ in the dark to 100 Ω in full light. The thermistor is an NTC one, 10 kΩ at 25 °C.
Diodes and LEDs
Conduct when the arrow points the way of the current, once the forward voltage is reached.
Capacitors and inductors
Time constants are RC and L/R. Give an inductor a coil resistance to model a real one. In AC circuits they have reactances 1/(2πfC) and 2πfL.
Transformer
Vs/Vp = Ns/Np. It only transforms a changing current; on DC its primary acts as a coil.
Fuse
Blows as soon as the current goes over its rating. Reset, or Replace in its panel, mends it.
