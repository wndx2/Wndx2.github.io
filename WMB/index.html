    What can I type?

    Names

    Systematic names
    Chains up to twelve carbons, rings, double and triple bonds, side groups and the common functional groups. 2-methylpropan-2-ol ethyl ethanoate
    Everyday names
    Common molecules, acids and ions. water sulfate aspirin

    Formulas

    Molecules and ions
    The Lewis structure is worked out from the electron count. Write a charge after a space or a caret. PCl5 SO4 2- NH4+
    Salts
    Drawn as separate ions in square brackets. NaCl Ca(OH)2
    Condensed formulas
    Atoms in the order they're bonded. A formula that fits several molecules, like C2H6O, has to be written this way or named. CH3CH(OH)CH3 (CH3)3COH

    SMILES

    Anything else
    Rings, aromatic rings and charged atoms. CC(=O)Oc1ccccc1C(=O)O [NH4+]

    Diagrams

    Lewis
    Every atom with its lone pairs. Bonds can be lines, pairs of dots, or dots and crosses, where each atom's own electrons share a symbol.
    Structural, skeletal, condensed
    All atoms and bonds; carbon chains as zigzags with hydrogens left out; or a line of text like CH3CH(OH)CH3.
    Shape
    The molecule in three dimensions around its central atom, with wedges coming towards you and dashes going away, and its shape, bond angle and polarity named underneath. For a larger molecule the shape around each atom is listed instead. NH3 SF4 XeF4

    Options

    Partial charges
    Marks δ+ and δ− on the atoms at either end of a polar bond.
    Resonance forms
    Draws every equally good arrangement of the electrons side by side. Works for formulas, where the electrons are worked out. NO3- O3 CH3COO-
    Expanded octets
    Off, every atom keeps to eight electrons where it can. On, atoms from the third period down take extra bonds to remove formal charges, so sulfate gets two S=O.
    Charges
    Auto puts an ion in square brackets with its overall charge and marks formal charges on neutral molecules.
